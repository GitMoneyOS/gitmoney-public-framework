# Standard Operating Procedure: Client Onboarding

**Document ID:** SOP-OPS-001  
**Owner:** Sarah Connor  
**Effective Date:** 2026-09-20  
**Version:** 1.0  

---

## Purpose
Ensure every new design client is fully onboarded within 48 hours of contract execution without requiring founder involvement.

## Phase 1: Intake & Folder Creation (Hour 0 to 4)
1. Project manager creates private client folder in shared drive.
2. Initialize client dashboard using the agency project template.
3. Invite client stakeholders to designated Slack channel.

## Phase 2: Kickoff Scheduling (Hour 4 to 12)
1. Send automated calendar invitation for 45-minute kickoff call.
2. Attach the pre-kickoff discovery questionnaire.

## Phase 3: Asset Collection (Hour 12 to 24)
1. Request brand guidelines, vector logos, and typography files.
2. Confirm receipt and archive assets in `01_intake/assets/`.

## Phase 4: Sprint Alignment (Hour 24 to 36)
1. Account lead conducts kickoff call.
2. Draft Milestone 1 delivery schedule.

## Phase 5: Handoff to Production (Hour 36 to 48)
1. Assign design tasks in project board.
2. Notify client that production sprint has commenced.
