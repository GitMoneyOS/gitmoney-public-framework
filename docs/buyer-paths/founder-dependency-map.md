# Founder Dependency Map

## Purpose

Map the buyer's operating wound to the public proof object and the paid path.

GitMoney OS should not make a serious buyer think, "Interesting repo." It should make them think, "This is the exact shape of a problem I already have, and the public layer is only the visible skeleton."

## Plain-English Job

Help a founder or executive diagnose whether their business is trapped in Founder-as-Middleware syndrome, identify which public proof artifact demonstrates the solution, and clarify when the wound requires a paid diagnostic or architecture build.

## Founder as Middleware

Founder dependency is not merely a time-management problem. It is a record, authority, context, handoff, and proof problem.

A founder becomes middleware when the business cannot reliably answer:

- where the source material lives;
- who owns the decision;
- what context is allowed;
- what stage the work is in;
- what evidence proves completion;
- who approves the next change;
- what breaks if the founder leaves the room.

## Before / After

| Operating Dimension | Before: Founder as Middleware | After: GitMoney OS Court of Record |

| Team Handoffs | Fragile; work breaks when someone leaves | Durable; reviewable pull requests and receipts |
